# Personas

## Persona: Complaint Analyst

### Purpose 
Investigate customer complaints, validate evidence, determine appropriate next steps, and prepare recommendations using the Banking Complaint Resolution Assistant before submitting cases for review. 

### Responsibilities
- Review complaint submissions
- Validate complaint details and required intake information
- Investigate customer, account, complaint, and service request history
- Review evidence retrieved by the Banking Complaint Resolution Assistant
- Review approved policies and procedures
- Validate complaint classifications, urgency, and evidence
- Prepare evidence packages and recommendation drafts
- Escalate sensitive, high-risk, or uncertain cases
- Maintain investigation records and auditability
- Support the complaint review workflow

### Goals
- Resolve complaints accurately
- Reduce investigation time
- Prepare evidence-based recommendations
- Identify missing or conflicting information before review
- Ensure recommendations are traceable to supporting evidence
- Improve customer experience

### Typical Tasks
1. Review incoming complaint
2. Validate intake information
3. Determine urgency
4. Review customer, account, and service request information
5. Review evidence retrieved by the assistant
6. Consult applicable policies and procedures
7. Prepare an evidence package
8. Draft a recommendation
9. Escalate cases when required
10. Submit cases for review

### Information Needs
- Customer and account information.
- Transaction details and service request history.
- Complaint category definitions.
- Urgency rules and escalation criteria.
- Approved policies, procedures, and guidance documents.
- Audit history for the complaint. 

### Permissions
- View complaint records
- View customer, account, and complaint information required for complaint handling
- Create recommendation drafts
- Submit cases for review
- Request escalation
- Update authorized case information

### Constraints
- Cannot approve reimbursements, compensation, or liability decisions
- Cannot approve sensitive outcomes
- Must rely on approved evidence and authorized data sources
- Must follow least-privilege access principles
- Must not modify original complaint narratives
- Must preserve complaint narratives without modification

## Success Measurements
 
- Recommendations are supported by evidence and citations 
- Sensitive cases are escalated appropriately
- Investigation activities are fully traceable and auditable
- Reduced investigation effort
- Accurate categorization

# Persona: Supervisor / Reviewer
 
## Purpose
Review escalated, sensitive, or uncertain complaint cases and make final approval decisions using the evidence, recommendations, and audit information provided through the Banking Complaint Resolution Assistant. 

## Responsibilities
- Review analyst recommendations
- Review supporting evidence and citations
- Validate policy compliance
- Approve or reject recommendations
- Maintain consistency and quality across complaint resolution decisions.
- Monitor complaint quality

## Goals

- Make informed and defensible decisions
- Protect customers and the organization from inappropriate outcomes
- Ensure recommendations are supported by sufficient evidence
- Reduce decision risk
- Maintain traceable and auditable decision making

## Tasks
1. Review escalation requests
2. Review the analyst's recommendation
3. Review supporting facts and evidence
4. Review policy and procedure references
5. Approve, reject, or request changes
6. Record decision rationale

## Information Needs
- Complete complaint details and complaint history.
- Complaint Analyst investigation recommandation.
- Supporting evidence gathered during the investigation.
- Approved policy, procedure, and escalation guidance citations.
- Audit information

## Permissions
- View recommendation drafts
- Approve recommendations
- Reject recommendations
- Request additional investigation

## Constraints
- Must justify review decisions
- Must follow approved policies and procedures
- Cannot approve unsupported recommendations
- Must maintain a complete audit trail

## Success Measurements
- Missing information and conflicting evidence are addressed before approval
- Consistent decision quality
- Compliance adherence
- Reduced risk exposure
- Review outcomes are reproducible and auditable 
- Complaint outcomes can be explained and justified using documented evidence
